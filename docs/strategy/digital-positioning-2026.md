# Blackburn Studio digital positioning — September 2026

## Purpose

Blackburn Studio should not compete as a commodity website provider.

Local Facebook and community-group research shows a crowded market with many designers offering websites, often with similar WordPress-led packages. The studio needs to be clear about the wider value it provides rather than competing on page count, platform choice or lowest price.

The positioning should make it clear that Blackburn Studio starts with the business problem and uses websites, digital systems, photography and technical support as tools to solve it.

## Positioning

**Blackburn Studio helps small businesses improve how they are found, understood and contacted online, and how the digital systems behind the business work.**

A website may be part of the answer, but it is not automatically the answer.

The studio's advantage is the combination of:

- practical business and engineering experience;
- more than 26 years in engineering, including leading teams around the world;
- hands-on website and digital delivery;
- workflow, Microsoft 365 and automation capability;
- photography and content creation in the same studio;
- local knowledge and direct accountability;
- managed technical support beyond initial launch.

## Background / trust copy

> Before starting Blackburn Studio, I spent more than 26 years working in engineering, leading teams around the world. That background shapes the way I approach digital work today: understand the problem first, work out what is actually getting in the way, then build the simplest solution that does the job properly.

This should be visible in the Digital journey and About page rather than being buried as secondary biography.

## Messaging principles

### Start with the problem

Avoid prescribing a website before understanding what is getting in the way.

Useful language:

- What is not working?
- What are customers finding difficult?
- Where are enquiries being lost?
- What takes more time than it should?
- What can be improved without rebuilding everything?

### Talk about outcomes before technology

Prefer:

- being easier to find;
- presenting the business clearly;
- making it easier for customers to make contact;
- improving enquiry and follow-up paths;
- simplifying repetitive work;
- reducing technical burden.

Technology should support the outcome rather than define the offer.

### Make the technical work easier for the client

A consistent Blackburn Studio promise is to make the process as easy as possible for the client.

Where appropriate, Blackburn Studio can handle the website, domain, hosting, DNS, forms, analytics, Microsoft 365, photography, integrations and ongoing support rather than leaving the client to coordinate multiple providers.

### Improve before rebuilding

Existing sites and systems should be improved when that is the better answer.

Do not position every visible issue as evidence that a complete rebuild is required.

### Do not compete by attacking WordPress

WordPress is a legitimate platform and may be appropriate for some projects.

Blackburn Studio should not position itself as better simply because it uses different technology. The differentiation is the way the problem is understood, the breadth of capability around the solution and the quality of ongoing support.

## Service structure

The Digital offering should increasingly be explained through outcomes:

### Websites and digital presence

New websites, rebuilds and practical improvements to how a business is found and understood online.

### Domains, hosting and technical setup

Domains, hosting, DNS, SSL and migrations handled without unnecessary technical burden for the client.

### Workflow and business systems

Microsoft 365, forms, SharePoint, workflow automation and custom tools that reduce friction in day-to-day work.

### Ongoing digital support

Maintenance, troubleshooting, administration and continued improvement.

### Digital Health Check

The Digital Health Check will become the diagnostic entry point for businesses that know something needs improving but do not yet know what the right solution is.

It should be launched after this positioning is established publicly.

## Primary CTA direction

Prefer low-pressure problem-led CTAs such as:

- Talk through what you need
- Tell me what is getting in the way
- Start a conversation

Avoid forcing visitors to choose a technical solution before they have discussed the problem.

## Website sequence

1. Publish the positioning update across Home, Digital and About.
2. Validate that the revised message is clear and consistent.
3. Finalise the Digital Health Check scope, scoring model, deliverable and price.
4. Add the Health Check as a clear entry service within the Digital journey.
5. Use community-group lead signals and outreach results to refine the offer based on real enquiries.

## Guardrail

The aim is not to make Blackburn Studio sound like a management consultancy.

The language should remain practical, local and grounded. Visitors should quickly understand that Mark can still design and build a website, but they should also understand that the website is one part of a broader ability to solve useful digital problems.
